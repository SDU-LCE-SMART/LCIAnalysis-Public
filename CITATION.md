# How to cite LCIAnalysis

**Citation metadata pending author approval and Zenodo DOI registration.**

Suggested citation structure (do not use as a final bibliographic record):

> [Approved author(s)]. ([Year]). *LCIAnalysis* (Version 2.0.0) [Computer software]. [Approved publisher / institutional attribution]. [Zenodo DOI after issuance].

The publication release should include an approved `CITATION.cff`, with author names/ORCIDs, publication date, repository URL, license identifier (if applicable), and Zenodo DOI only after they are confirmed. Avoid fabricated author names or identifiers.
