# Scientific scope and limitations

LCIAnalysis supports LCI development and scientific review; it is not itself a fully validated LCA impact-assessment engine.

- Preserve original units, source cells, reported/estimated status and explicit assumptions.
- Treat numerical mass-balance results as consistency checks; account for unknown streams, losses, accumulation and basis differences.
- Review stream composition, allocations, system boundaries and conversion factors before approving connections.
- Energy and heat calculations require appropriate temperature, pressure, composition, phase, property and operating-time information.
- System scaling does not automatically prove scale-invariant yield, equipment capacity or energy intensity.
- Synthetic examples and UI screenshots do not establish validation on arbitrary industrial cases.

Scientific validation and software functional acceptance should be documented separately for each approved release.
